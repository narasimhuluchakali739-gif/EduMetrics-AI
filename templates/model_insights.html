Model Limitations & Dataset Scope
Technical documentation of constraints and boundary conditions
Synthetic Cohort Disclaimer

The model was trained on a synthesized benchmark cohort (N=2,500) incorporating non-linear functions established in educational data mining literature. While realistic, predictions demonstrate software engineering and statistical workflows rather than definitive medical or clinical certainty.

Omitted Qualitative Variables

The feature set strictly excludes sensitive demographic and socioeconomic variables (gender, ethnicity, household income, geographic location) to prevent institutional demographic bias. Important unmeasured qualitative factors such as sudden personal illness, examination anxiety, or family emergencies cannot be captured by the regression model.

Uncertainty & Confidence Interval Reporting

To protect students from deterministic assumptions, all predictions provide explicit 90% confidence bands based on residual root mean squared variance (±3.1 marks on a 100-point scale).
